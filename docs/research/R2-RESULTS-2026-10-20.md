## Regression caught by the full suite
The deferred-encoding catch-up in `query()` took a store read lock, which blocked a reader behind a
held write lock during compaction (`mutation_gate::tests::queries_progress_while_compaction_waits_and_a_mutation_is_queued`,
failing deterministically on the branch, passing on main). Fixed with `try_read`: a contended lock
means no catch-up on that query. Suites rerun after the fix.

## Why the server was slow
`exact+index` only built a bounding-box tree (`src/core/documents/dense.rs`) whose bound prunes
nothing at 384-d; the qgraph index had no engine or server caller; each query cloned 50k `Arc`s and
used f64 dots. The new `VectorIndex` trait (`src/core/documents/vector_index.rs`) searches a shared
immutable snapshot, re-scores graph candidates exactly, and leaves a slot for the research scan.

## ANN at 1M+: rare-witness decoding, KILLED
Direction: occupancy-balanced rare-witness decoding (signed projection bank, tail-quantile
memberships with equal channel occupancy, threshold-join of posting streams, bulk rerank), chosen because candidate discovery needs no dependent random fetches. Prototype
`benchmarks/research/witness/` (compiled first time). Stage-1 gate on glove-100 (1.18M vectors,
1,000 reporting queries, 36 configurations over M in {4096, 32768}, B in {16, 24, 32}, Q in {16, 32,
64}, t in {2, 3}): 0 of 36 pass. Best candidate recall@10 is 0.83 (M=4096, B=32, Q=64, t=3) at a
median 36k candidates and 2.4 MB of posting bytes per query, against a gate of 0.98 at 1,000
candidates and 100 KB; inside the budget (<= 100 KB) the best is 0.30. Rank-10 survival rises with
true similarity (0.95 to 1.0 above cosine 0.7) but glove tenth neighbours sit at 0.4 to 0.6, where
survival is 0.1 to 0.5. The predicted failure mode (tenth neighbours share too few extreme witnesses) is what happened. The 200k smoke and nytimes-256 runs agree. Killed; the
survival-by-similarity curve is the evidence for the next attempt. Large-N ANN against FAISS stays open.
